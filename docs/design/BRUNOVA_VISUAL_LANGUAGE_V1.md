# Brunova Visual Language v1.0

**Status:** Approved design direction<br>
**Scope:** Brunova Website and future digital experiences<br>
**Owner:** Brunova<br>
**Purpose:** Define the visual language used to communicate Brunova's systems engineering and operational intelligence positioning.

---

# 1. Purpose

Brunova does not present itself as an automation vendor, software agency, or AI consultancy.

Brunova designs and builds reliable systems for operations that have outgrown their existing tools, processes, and fragmented workflows.

The visual language must communicate:

- systems thinking;
- operational understanding;
- architectural discipline;
- controlled transformation;
- technical credibility;
- executive confidence.

The goal is not to make complex systems look simple.

The goal is to make complex systems understandable.

---

# 2. Core Visual Principle

## From fragmentation to operational systems

Every Brunova visual should communicate a transformation:

Fragmented operation
    ↓
Defined operating model
    ↓
Reliable operation

The visual language is built around the idea that businesses rarely fail because they lack tools.

They fail because:

- boundaries are unclear;
- ownership is undefined;
- decisions are implicit;
- operational state is unreliable;
- systems grow without architecture.

Brunova creates the structure that makes operations understandable and controllable.

# 4. Enterprise Positioning

"Fortune 500 quality" does not mean copying enterprise websites.

It means:

- exceptional hierarchy;
- strong editorial judgment;
- intentional whitespace;
- visual restraint;
- meaningful systems visualization;
- premium typography;
- confidence without exaggeration.

Brunova should communicate:

> "There is a serious engineering discipline behind this company."

Not:

> "This company has many services."

---

# 5. Visual Narrative Model

All major Brunova experiences should follow:

Recognition
      ↓
Understanding
      ↓
Confidence
      ↓
Conversation

The user should first recognize their operational problem.

Then understand Brunova's model.

Then trust the approach.

Then engage.

# 6. Primary Visual Concepts

## 6.1 System Boundary

### Meaning

Represents:

- architecture;
- ownership;
- responsibility;
- controlled scope.

A Brunova system is not a collection of tools.

It is a defined operational boundary.

Example:

┌─────────────────────┐
│                     │
│   Operating Model   │
│                     │
│   State             │
│   Decisions         │
│   Ownership         │
│                     │
└─────────────────────┘

Use for:

- Hero;
- Architecture sections;
- Work dossiers.

Avoid:

- random boxes;
- disconnected diagrams;
- infrastructure diagrams without business meaning.

---

## 6.2 Transformation Flow

### Meaning

Represents operational change.

Pattern:

A  ─────────→  B  ─────────→  C

Examples:

Fragmented
Defined
Reliable

Use for:

- Process;
- Engagement models;
- Before/after explanations.

Avoid:

- generic timelines;
- mandatory funnels.

---

## 6.3 Operational State

### Meaning

Represents maturity and control.

Examples:

Unknown
↓
Defined
↓
Reliable

Use when explaining:

- discovery;
- evolution;
- operational maturity.

---

## 6.4 Operational Intelligence Control Loop

### Meaning

Operational Intelligence is Brunova's conceptual framework.

It is not an AI capability.

It is the ability to understand operational state, make governed decisions, and execute reliable action.

Visual model:

          OBSERVE

  Operations
  Data
  Systems


          ↓


          DECIDE

  Meaning
  Boundaries
  Ownership


          ↓


           ACT

  Automation
  Software
  AI


          ↓


  Operational state
      returns

Important:

AI is a governed execution capability.

AI is not the center of the system.

---

## 6.5 Capability Layers

### Meaning

Capabilities are not independent services.

They are engineering disciplines that combine into operational systems.

Visual concept:

Operational System
Systems Architecture
Data & Integration
Financial Operations
Workflow Engineering
Automation Modernization

Avoid:

- five disconnected service cards;
- feature grids;
- SaaS pricing-style layouts.

---

## 6.6 Evidence Structure

### Meaning

Selected work represents engineered systems, not portfolio items.

The visual pattern:

System
Context
Boundary
Engineering decisions
Outcome

Avoid:

- agency portfolio cards;
- client logo walls;
- fake metrics;
- testimonials without approval.

# 8. Typography

Typography remains:

## Display

Manrope

Used for:

- major statements;
- hero;
- section titles.

## Body/UI

IBM Plex Sans

Used for:

- explanations;
- navigation;
- metadata;
- supporting content.

Rules:

- prioritize hierarchy over volume;
- use weight and spacing;
- avoid unnecessary bold;
- avoid excessive uppercase;
- avoid decorative typography.

---

# 9. Color System

The visual language remains:

## Primary

- Black
- Near black
- Bone
- Warm white
- Warm gray

Avoid:

- blue technology aesthetics;
- purple AI aesthetics;
- neon accents;
- gradients.

Color should communicate:

- seriousness;
- precision;
- stability.

Not:

- innovation hype.

---

# 10. Logo Treatment

The Brunova logo should be treated as a brand signature.

The issue is not the logo itself.

The issue is integration.

The logo should feel:

- intentional;
- embedded;
- premium.

Avoid:

- image pasted into header;
- arbitrary black rectangle treatment;
- recreated typography.

Preferred future directions:

## Wordmark signature

BRUNOVA
Systems Engineering
Operational Intelligence

## Architectural boundary

A subtle frame representing:

- precision;
- system boundaries;
- structure.

The logo must remain unchanged unless official brand assets are approved.

---

# 11. Navigation and Wayfinding

Navigation must answer:

- Where am I?
- What belongs here?
- Where can I go next?

Use:

- active navigation;
- contextual labels;
- route relationships;
- subtle trails.

Avoid:

- application sidebars;
- large breadcrumb bars;
- heavy navigation chrome.

---

# 12. Homepage Visual Direction

The homepage is the primary expression of Brunova.

It must communicate:

Problem recognition
    ↓
Operational Intelligence
    ↓
Engineering capabilities
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Engagement

The homepage should not explain everything.

It should create confidence.

---

# 13. Visual Density Rules

More information does not equal more credibility.

Prefer:

One strong idea

over:

Several explanations.

Primary pages should generally contain:

- strong headline;
- concise supporting thought;
- meaningful visual structure.

Detailed explanations belong deeper in the site.

---

# 14. Asset Creation Rules

Visual assets should be:

- semantic;
- reusable;
- brand-specific;
- meaningful.

Preferred formats:

- SVG;
- CSS-based systems;
- geometric compositions.

Avoid:

- stock images;
- generic illustrations;
- AI artwork;
- decorative assets without meaning.

# 16. AI Usage Principle

AI is part of Brunova's capabilities.

AI is not Brunova's identity.

The visual language must avoid:

- AI brains;
- neural networks;
- glowing nodes;
- futuristic interfaces.

AI belongs inside governed systems.

---

# 17. Final Design Test

Before approving any Brunova visual ask:

## Does this explain a system?

If yes, continue.

## Does this only decorate?

Remove.

## Does this make Brunova look like another AI company?

Reject.

## Does this help an executive understand complexity?

Keep.

---

# Final Principle

Brunova visuals should make complex operations feel understandable, not simple.

The goal is not to impress through decoration.

The goal is to demonstrate control.
