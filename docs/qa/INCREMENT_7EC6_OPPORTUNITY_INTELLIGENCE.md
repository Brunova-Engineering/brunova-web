# Increment 7E-C.6 — Opportunity Intelligence + Executive UX

Status: **technical/product implementation complete; Human Portal Acceptance pending**.

The Portal now derives a company-specific decision memo from one authoritative
Engine Discovery read. The projection is presentation-only: it does not rewrite
Candidate evidence, source observations, historical outcomes, provenance, or
workspace state.

## Decision memo contract

Each Opportunity presents, before technical disclosure:

- an evidence-bounded company summary;
- concrete reasons the organization remains relevant;
- material facts that remain unproven;
- the Engine's current route and the condition that would change it;
- a presentation stage plus current and next decision dimensions;
- one exact next action with purpose, expected decision change, and stop
  condition;
- a separate continue, hold, wait, Management-review, or archive recommendation.

Canonical Engine text and evidence remain available behind progressive
disclosure. An archive recommendation never executes the governed archive
command.
